### Direct billing
The clinic bills 20+ extended health insurers directly, including Pacific Blue Cross, Medavie Blue Cross/RCMP, Green Shield, Great-West Life and Sun Life. The clinic verifies coverage and the patient pays only the uncovered balance.

### ICBC (motor vehicle accident claims)
ICBC covers chiropractic, massage therapy, physiotherapy, acupuncture and active rehab.
1. Report the claim to ICBC (604-520-8222) to get a claim number.
2. Submit the intake form at joayotherapy.ca/service/icbc/, or call or email the clinic.

There is typically no out-of-pocket cost. The user fees listed on Jane App are $15 (45min standard RMT), $35 (60min extended RMT) and $10 (extended physio follow-up).
