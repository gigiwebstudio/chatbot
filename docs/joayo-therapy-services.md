## Billing

### Direct billing
20+ extended health insurers (Pacific Blue Cross, Medavie Blue Cross/RCMP, Green Shield, Great-West Life, Sun Life). Clinic verifies coverage, patient pays no upfront cost, clinic bills insurer directly, patient pays only uncovered balance.

### ICBC (motor vehicle accident claims)
Covers chiropractic, massage therapy, physiotherapy, acupuncture, active rehab, clinical counseling.
Steps: (1) report claim to ICBC (604-520-8222) for a claim number, (2) submit intake form at joayotherapy.ca/service/icbc/ or call/email the clinic.
Typically no out-of-pocket cost; occasional $10-$35 user fee on certain follow-up sessions.
